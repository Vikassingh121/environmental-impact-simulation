📐 Simulation Methodology & Data Sources
  • Zoning Strategy: Each 2-acre (8,094 sq m) micro-sanctuary is strictly zoned: ~1 acre for dense Miyawaki forest (shade/oxygen), ~0.5 acre for open grazing meadow, ~8% for a deep water body, and the rest for animal resting paths.
  • Miyawaki Forest Density: 3,000 native saplings planted per acre of forest zone, creating a multi-layered canopy that matures in 3-5 years.
  • Temperature Reduction: Based on IIT Kanpur research, mature Miyawaki forests reduce local temperatures by a minimum of 2°C and up to 8°C depending on density.
  • PM2.5 Filtration: Dense tree canopies trap 7-24% of particulate matter in a 500m radius (Nature Sustainability, 2022). We use a conservative 15% reduction per micro-sanctuary zone.
  • Evapotranspiration Cooling: Each large tree transpires ~400 liters/day in summer, producing cooling equivalent to 2 room ACs (US EPA). Scaled by tree density.
  • Gas Composition: Atmospheric baselines from NOAA. Local deviations modeled using urban pollution gradients and photosynthesis rates per canopy area.
  • Micro-Sanctuary Count: Calculated as city area ÷ (π × 2²) = area ÷ 12.57 km² per sanctuary zone (non-overlapping circles in a 2km radius grid).
  • Note: This is an indicative model based on The Accountability Manifesto v2. Numbers represent scientifically-grounded estimates to demonstrate the massive potential of the Coexistence Mandate at scale.
